Getting started

Next! is a single file that opens in your web browser. No account, no install, and no internet needed once it's open.

  1. Unzip the download. On Windows, right-click the ZIP file and choose Extract All. On a Mac, double-click it. On a Chromebook, open the ZIP in the Files app and copy the file out. Opening the file while it's still inside the ZIP can keep it from saving.
  2. Put the file somewhere permanent. Move Next-Classroom-Line.html to your Documents folder or Desktop (on a Chromebook, My files). Avoid Downloads, which some computers clean out. Keep it in the same place from now on.
  3. Double-click to open it in Chrome or Edge. If it opens in a different browser, right-click the file and choose Open with › Google Chrome (or Microsoft Edge).
  4. Make it one click away. Bookmark the page or drag it to your bookmarks bar so it's easy to open every morning.
  5. Set up your class. Tap Settings and create your 4-digit teacher PIN. Then name your classes, choose typed names or rosters, and set your hall pass rules, including the places students can go.
  6. Show your students. Drag the ticket left for help or right for a hall pass. Tap How it works anytime to show the welcome screen again.

Tip: The example students on the board leave as soon as a real student takes a ticket, or tap Start my class.

Basics

Do I need internet, an account, or a website?

No. Next! is one HTML file that runs in your browser. Double-click it and it opens like a web page. You don't need to upload it anywhere. Internet is only used for the fonts and the optional Google Sheets feature.

Which browsers and devices work?
  • Best: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a Windows PC, Mac, Chromebook, or interactive board connected to one.
  • Usually fine: Firefox and Safari, but Chrome or Edge are recommended.
  • iPads and phones: opening a downloaded HTML file on an iPad is unreliable. To use Next! on an iPad, put it on a Google Site (see below) and open that page instead.
My district blocks opening files in Chrome. What can I do?

A few districts lock down Chrome so it can't open files from the computer. You can put Next! on a Google Site instead:

  1. Open the Next! file in a text editor (Notepad on Windows, TextEdit on a Mac) and copy everything.
  2. In Google Sites, choose Insert › Embed › Embed code, paste, and click Next then Insert.
  3. Make the box as large as the page, then publish the site and open it on your board.

Inside Google Sites, some browsers and school accounts don't keep saved data as reliably. Open Reports and check the saving message at the bottom, and save a backup file each week.

Can I use Next! on more than one computer?

Yes, but each computer and browser keeps its own records. To move everything to another computer, use a backup file (see Saving and your data). To collect records from several computers in one place, use Google Sheets auto-send or download a spreadsheet from each one.

Can I use it with more than one class or period?

Yes, up to 7. Name them in Settings › Classes. When a new class starts, pick it from the Class menu at the top of the board. If students are still in line from the last class, Next! asks whether to keep them or clear the line. Reports can be filtered by class.

Teacher PIN

What is the teacher PIN for?

The 4-digit PIN keeps students out of Settings and Reports, and out of tapping another student to send or remove them. After you enter it, teacher mode stays unlocked for 5 minutes. Tap Teacher mode · tap to lock to lock it sooner.

I forgot my PIN.

On the PIN screen, tap Forgot PIN? then Erase and reset. To protect student privacy, this erases the PIN, settings, rosters and every report on that computer. There is no way around this, and Bellwork Labs can't see or recover your data.

If you have a backup file, set a new PIN, then open Reports › Restore backup › Open a backup file to bring your classes and records back.

Students and lines

Should students type their names or use a roster?

Typed names: students type their first name (you can also ask for a last initial). The keyboard has letters only, and inappropriate words are blocked.

Rosters: students tap their name from a list. In Settings › Student names, choose Roster, then paste names or upload a CSV or text file for each class. Rosters avoid typos and make reports cleaner.

How do I send a student now, move them up, or take them out of line?

Tap the student on the board (or in the list) and enter your PIN. For a hall pass, choose Send now or Remove from line. For the help line, choose Help next to move them to the front, or Remove from line. Students sent early are still timed, and reports mark them "Sent by teacher."

A student forgot to tap I'm back.

Tap I'm back for them, or open Reports › Now and tap Mark back next to their name. The trip time ends when you tap, so the report may show a longer trip.

How do I limit hall passes?

In Settings › Hall pass rules you can:

  • Set how many students can be out at once (1 to 5). Others wait in line and go automatically.
  • Close passes for the first and last 5, 10 or 15 minutes of class. Set each class's start and end times in Settings › Classes.
  • Limit trips per student per day or per week. You can override the limit with your PIN.
  • Pick the places students can go, and which ones skip the line.

To flag long trips, choose a time limit in Settings › Display. Timers turn amber at that time and red at double.

Why do the Nurse and Office skip the line?

Some places shouldn't wait. In Settings › Hall pass rules, places listed under "Go now" let a student leave right away and don't count toward the out-at-once limit. You can turn any place on or off.

Display and customizing

Can I turn off the characters and just show a list?

Yes. Choose Settings › Display › Board style › List, or tap List view in the top bar. Switch back anytime. Students in line stay put.

Can I add my school's logo and colors?

Yes. In Settings › Make it yours, add your school name, upload a logo (PNG or JPG works best), and pick primary and secondary colors. The logo appears on the classroom wall and in the top bar, and the teacher's outfit matches your primary color. Reset colors goes back to the original look.

Can I turn off the sounds or animations?

Yes, in Settings › Display. Turning off animations gives a calmer board for students who are easily distracted.

Saving and your data

Where is my data stored? Who can see it?

Everything is saved inside your browser on that one computer. Nothing is sent to Bellwork Labs. Records only leave the computer if you download a spreadsheet, save a backup file, or turn on Google Sheets auto-send to your own Google account.

Next! asks for first names only and has no student logins. Check your school's rules for storing student information.

My reports, settings or rosters disappeared.

Saved data belongs to one browser on one computer. The usual causes:

  • The browser's history or site data was cleared. Some school computers do this every night.
  • Next! was opened in a different browser, a different Chrome profile, or a private (incognito) window.
  • The file was opened from inside the ZIP, or moved or renamed.
  • On a Google Site, the embed was replaced or edited.

To get it back, open your most recent backup file (Reports › Restore backup › Open a backup file). To check whether saving works on your computer, open Reports and read the message at the bottom.

How do I back up, and how often?

Open Reports and tap Save backup file. It saves your settings, rosters, branding and every record. Keep it in Google Drive or OneDrive. Once a week is a good habit, and always before you clear your browser or update Next!.

To restore, tap Restore backup › Open a backup file. Records you already have are skipped, so restoring twice won't create duplicates.

How do I move Next! to a new computer?
  1. On the old computer, tap Reports › Save backup file.
  2. Copy the Next! file and the backup file to the new computer (a flash drive, Drive or OneDrive works).
  3. Open Next! on the new computer, create a PIN, and use Restore backup › Open a backup file.
How do I erase student data at the end of the year?

Download a spreadsheet first if you need to keep a record. Then open Reports and tap Erase log. To also clear rosters, remove them in Settings › Student names. To erase everything, including your PIN and settings, use Forgot PIN? › Erase and reset.

Reports and spreadsheets

What do the reports show?

Reports has four tabs: Now (who's out and waiting), Hall passes, Help line, and Students. Tap any student's name for their own report: trips, minutes out, favorite places, time of day, help sessions, and how they compare to the class average. Filter by date range, class and place.

How do I get my records into Excel or Google Sheets?

Open Reports and tap Download spreadsheet. You get an Excel file (.xlsx) with three tabs: Hall passes, Help line and Students. It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers. Choose Since my last download to get only new rows, which makes it easy to add them to a running spreadsheet.

You can also tap Copy for a spreadsheet and paste right into any spreadsheet.

The download button doesn't do anything.

Downloads can be blocked when Next! runs inside another page, like a Google Site or a preview window. Open the Next! file directly in Chrome or Edge and try again. Also check your browser's downloads list. The file may have saved without a pop-up.

Google Sheets auto-send

What is auto-send, and do I need it?

It's an optional bonus for schools that use Google. Once it's set up, every finished hall pass and help session is added to a Google Sheet in your own Drive automatically. It's handy for sharing with an administrator or case manager, or as an always-current backup of your records. If you don't use Google, the Download spreadsheet button does the same job by hand.

How do I set it up?
  1. In Google Drive, make a new Google Sheet. Name it something like Next! records.
  2. In that sheet, click Extensions › Apps Script.
  3. In Next!, open Settings › Send to Google Sheets and tap Copy setup code. Back in Apps Script, delete what's there, paste, and click the Save icon.
  4. Click Deploy › New deployment. Click the gear and pick Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone (or your school's option). Click Deploy.
  5. Click Authorize access and choose your account.
  6. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec), paste it into Next!, and tap Test connection.

To send records you already have, tap Send my past records.

Google says "Google hasn't verified this app." Is that safe?

Yes. Google shows this for any script you make yourself, since you are the app's author. Click Advanced, then Go to your project, then Allow. The script can only add rows to that one spreadsheet. It can't read or change anything else in your Drive.

Test connection says it couldn't connect.
  • Make sure you copied the Web app URL that ends in /exec, not the address of the spreadsheet or the script editor.
  • Check Who has access. If you chose your school's option instead of Anyone, the board's browser must be signed in to your school Google account.
  • If you changed the code after deploying, choose Deploy › Manage deployments, click the pencil, set Version to New version, and deploy again.
  • Some districts turn off Apps Script web apps. If the steps above don't help, ask your tech department, or use Download spreadsheet instead.
  • Next! won't connect inside a preview window. Open the file directly in Chrome or Edge.
What if the internet goes down?

Nothing is lost. Next! keeps records that couldn't be sent and tries again every minute. The status line in Settings shows how many are waiting.

Will I get duplicate rows?

No. Each row has a Record ID, and rows already in your sheet are skipped. Please don't delete or move the Record ID column. You can sort, filter, add columns to the right, or make new tabs for charts.

Can my whole team share one sheet?

Yes. One person sets up the sheet and shares the Web app URL with the team. Each teacher pastes it into Next! and fills in Room or teacher name so every row shows where it came from. Treat the URL like a password: anyone who has it can add rows (but can't read the sheet).

Does auto-send work with Microsoft Excel or OneDrive?

Not automatically. Google lets anyone make the small receiving script for free. Microsoft's version requires a premium license most schools don't have. Microsoft schools get the same records with Download spreadsheet, which opens right in Excel.

Updates

How do I get a new version?
  1. Open Next! and tap Reports › Save backup file, just in case.
  2. On Teachers Pay Teachers, go to My Purchases and download Next! again. Updates are free.
  3. Unzip it and put the new file in the same folder with the same name as your old one, replacing it.
  4. Open it as usual. Your settings, rosters and records will still be there. If anything is missing, use Restore backup › Open a backup file.

Using a Google Site? Replace the embed code with the new file, then restore your backup file, because a new embed starts with empty storage.
