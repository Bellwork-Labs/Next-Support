Allatoona Buccaneers · help line & hall passes
  1. 1Grab a doubloon. Drag it left to get help at the Captain's Quarters, or right for a hall pass. If only one is on, just tap it.
  2. 2Pick where you're going. The bathroom, water, vending, the media center or the hall. Nurse and Counseling skip the line.
  3. 3Add your name. Type your first name and last initial, or tap your name if your teacher uses a roster.
  4. 4Check back in. Your teacher taps I'm done after helping. Tap I'm back when you return.
  • Teachers: open Settings first to create your PIN. Reports, Settings and removing students all need it.
  • In Settings, turn on your blocks, choose typed names or rosters, and set hall pass rules like how many can be out at once.
  • Pick the current block at the top. Reports can be filtered by block.
  • Everything is saved only in this browser on this board. Use Save backup file in Reports to keep a copy.
